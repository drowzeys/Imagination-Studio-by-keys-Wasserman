## Imagination Studio key art

Generated for this app on 2026-10-17 with the built-in image generation tool of Sam Wasserman's ChatGPT desktop app
(the app does not name the image model). Invented content only: no real people, places, logos or text. The full-size
originals are kept outside the repo.

- art/home-hero.webp: an empty soundstage at night, a camera on a dolly, one amber work light
- art/engine-story.webp: a writer's desk with index cards, a clapperboard and a fountain pen
- art/engine-image.webp: a light table with contact sheets, a loupe and a large-format camera
- art/engine-video.webp: a cinema camera on a dolly track at blue hour
- art/engine-previs.webp: a grey-box miniature set with a camera path, a monitor of wireframes
- art/engine-sound.webp: a condenser microphone and amber VU meters in a vocal booth
- art/engine-edit.webp: an edit suite with a timeline, a reference monitor and a jog wheel
- art/start-clip.webp: a paper lantern on a wooden porch at dusk
- art/start-scout.webp: six printed frames pinned to a dark wall, one circled in grease pencil
- art/start-still.webp: a framed photograph under a picture light
- art/start-sharp.webp: a macro of a cinema prime lens and its focus scale
- art/start-long.webp: a long hotel corridor with receding practicals
- art/start-voice.webp: a microphone with a pop filter, headphones on a stand
- art/start-song.webp: an upright piano with sheet music under one lamp
- art/start-agent.webp: a drafting table with a set blueprint and a robot hand
- art/look-noir.webp: venetian-blind shadows on a wall
- art/look-golden.webp: backlit dust in a golden beam
- art/look-analog.webp: the sprocketed edge of a film frame
